Smart mobile medical-waste collection & segregation system

An AI-powered, battery-electric autonomous system that identifies, segregates, and digitally tracks biomedical waste in hospitals — reducing manual handling and improving compliance with waste management guidelines.

View operator screen

The problem

Hospitals generate large volumes of biomedical waste that must be correctly identified and segregated at the point of disposal. Manual sorting is slow, inconsistent, and puts staff at risk of exposure to hazardous material.

Our approach

A mobile unit scans each item with a camera, uses an AI model to identify what it is, and routes it into the correct waste category — sharps, anatomical, recyclable, or glass/metal — while logging every item for digital tracking.

What's built so far

The machine's interior and exterior are being designed in Autodesk Fusion, and the identification model is being trained and evaluated separately. This site includes a mockup of the operator screen that will show live scan results and bin status once the two are connected.
