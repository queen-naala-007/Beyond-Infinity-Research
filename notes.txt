14. Points Requiring Verification for further Field Study
•	Confirm which waste categories are suitable for autoclave treatment and which require other treatment routes.
•	Record the packing materials and whether packing covers are actually reusable after disinfection.
•	Understand the exact CTF receiving process after hospital collection.
•	Verify the downstream treatment-facility workflow before designing the treatment-facility extension.
•	Separate direct hospital observations from information obtained from reports, websites or secondary references.
15. Recommended Research Methodology
•	Observe first and avoid leading hospital staff toward the proposed solution.
•	Record the existing workflow as it actually occurs.
•	Keep each hospital's observations separate until comparison is complete.
•	Compare hospitals to identify repeated problems and process variations.
•	Convert repeated observations into system requirements.
•	Only after validation, finalize sensors, mechanical components and software features.
•	Prototype the hospital-side system first, then evaluate the need for a downstream treatment-facility extension.
